# Plan 04-01: Email Reminder Service & Adapters

## Objective
Implement transactional email notification dispatch for medication reminders and appointment alerts using Resend and local mock adapters.

## Tasks
1. Build email service (`src/services/notification-service.ts`) with pluggable transport (Resend API + Local Console/Memory Mock).
2. Create accessible HTML email templates for:
   - Medication dose reminders
   - Unresolved dose alert notification for caregivers
   - Appointment confirmation and reminders
3. Test email dispatch logic with mock logger.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Email transport dispatches formatted notifications.
- In test/dev mode, emails are cleanly recorded without network failure.
