# Plan 05-01: Caregiver Alert Claim Responsibility & Resolution

## Objective
Implement caregiver claim responsibility workflow to coordinate multi-caregiver family teams and prevent duplicate interventions.

## Tasks
1. Implement alert claiming action: updates alert status to `claimed`, records `claimed_by` and `claimed_at`.
2. Implement resolution action: caregiver adds resolution notes (e.g. "Called Mom, watched her swallow pill") and updates dose status to `taken`.
3. Emit audit logs for claim and resolution events.
4. Add unit tests for claim and resolution actions.

## Acceptance Criteria
- Only authorized caregivers can claim and resolve alerts.
- Claim prevents other caregivers from duplicating effort.
- Resolution notes recorded and dose state updated safely.
